Macarons

A sweet meringue-based confection made with egg whites, powdered sugar, granulated sugar, almond flour and food coloring.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups of almond flour
  • 2 cups of powdered sugar
  • 1/2 cups of granulated sugar
  • 4 egg whites
  • Food coloring

Instructions

  1. Sift the powdered sugar and almond flour three times to remove clumps;
  2. Preheat the oven to 140 ºC;
  3. Whisk powdered sugar and almond flour in a bowl;
  4. Beat egg whites with salt in a electric mixer until foamy, and then gradually add granulated sugar, until egg whites are glossy;
  5. Fold almond flour mixture into whipped egg whites until entirely homogeneous. If you want, you can add some food coloring. Spoon the merengue into a pastry bag and then pipe 1-inch disks of merengue onto the baking sheets;
  6. Let them stand for 30 minutes;
  7. Place the baking sheets inside the preheated oven and let them bake until dry for about 20 minutes. Let the macarons cool completely;
  8. Spread the macarons cookies with the desired filling. Refrigerate them for about 2 hours;
  9. Enjoy!
