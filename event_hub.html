Calgary Expo 2026

Everyone is a fan of something, and CALGARY EXPO is a place to celebrate all things related to pop culture. Get an autograph or a photo with your favourite Guest then get the inside scoop about your favourite movies and TV shows at our celebrity panels! Watch professional Comic Artists battle it out in our popular Sketch Duels, learn from our "How To" workshops, and take photos with your favourite costumed characters! It's also a great place to buy a unique gift (or treat yourself!) with a huge show floor for shopping madness.

Date: April 23-26, 2026
