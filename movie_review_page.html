Movie Review: Hunger Games

Hunger Games talks about a girl called Katniss Everdeen, who is from district 12. Her sister was selected to participate in The Hunger Games, which is kind of a battle royale against other players. To protect her sister, she volunteered to participate in the games. Her life turns upside-down considering how intense and violent the games really were. A story about opression and cruelty involving a society (capitol) that has already succumbed to their own madness.
