A trip to Japan

In Japan, you can discover a lot of things from culture, like manga, music, anime, to nature (sakuras - there is a famous event in Japan called hanami, with a lot of picnics under sakura trees - exotic birds and beautiful flowers). You can also visit temples, like kimkaku-ji and kiyomizu-dera.
